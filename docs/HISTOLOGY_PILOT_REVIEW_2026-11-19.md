This package prepares the existing epithelial-lining session for a scoped specialist review and a small learner pilot. No specialist has signed off this package and no learner outcomes have been collected.

## Learner session

Follow the existing protocol in PRELAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md. Begin with five unfamiliar first-year learners to find recurring problems. This convenience sample informs revision and does not establish population pass rates or product efficacy.

1. Before sessions, the specialist selects three comparable, appropriately licensed teaching fields that participants have not encountered in the session or its linked practice. Keep those fields and the assessment key in the controlled private review materials. Use a different field at baseline, immediately after study and one week later.
2. Define the accepted observations and a short rubric for each field before seeing participant responses. Each criterion receives zero or one point. Score whether the learner locates the relevant free surface or lumen, supports the classification using two observable structural clues, and states a justified limitation with a sensible next observation. Accept an appropriate expression of uncertainty where the field is insufficient.
3. Obtain consent. Record an anonymous session ID, relevant starting knowledge, device and any assistance. Keep names, responses and session notes out of Git and out of website analytics.
4. Ask the learner to find a beginner tissue-identification session from the homepage without coaching. Record the chosen links, wrong turns and requests for help separately from the learning tasks.
5. Collect the baseline explanation, let the learner complete the existing session, and collect an explanation of the second field. Keep the original response separate from later corrections. Record whether the saved-writing control, figure enlargement, comparison criteria and checkpoint feedback are understandable.
6. Check the existing 24-hour follow-up and one-week review on the learner's own browser. At one week, assess the third held-out field without showing its labels or assessment key. Record attendance and help separately. The site's reused practice images are teaching materials, so seeing them again is not an unseen outcome measure.
7. Have the specialist score the first responses. Report the actual numerator and denominator at each stage, the three rubric criteria, prior knowledge and losses to follow-up. A second scorer can independently score ambiguous responses and record disagreement.
8. Fix the first repeated navigation or explanation problem. Recheck the affected flow with different examples before broadening the pilot.
