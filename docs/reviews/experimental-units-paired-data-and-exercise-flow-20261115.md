# Editorial review of three introductory lessons

The owner approved release of the two Biostatistics lessons on experimental units and pairing, and the exercise-circulation lesson for the existing cardiovascular chapter. The public adaptations use original explanations, fictional data and attributed public references. No underlying course question, guide pages, or source illustration is reproduced.

- Paired differences. Checked the NIST pairing and variance setup, recomputed both fictional tables, and verified the covariance and standard-error explanation under the stated pair assumptions.
- Exercise circulation. Checked Revision 61, Part II §2.11, and human regional-flow research. Recomputed the fictional example and verified the difference between absolute flow, share of output and percentage points.
- Visuals. Three original HTML comparison tables have visible values, measurement units, an observation task and equivalent text.
