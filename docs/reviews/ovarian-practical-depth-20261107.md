# Ovarian practical and developmental evidence

The existing reproductive-histology overview lacks an ovarian photograph practical. This unit adds 21 original explanations, 24 original applied questions with 96 answer-choice explanations, three human ovarian fields and 12 region anchors. Two conceptual tracks each contain six prompts (12 prompts total), without a universal fetal timetable. Existing section-plane and staining prerequisites are reused rather than copied.

Jpogi released the small-follicle and corpus-albicans photographs into the public domain. Ed Uthman’s cumulus photograph is CC BY 2.0. The files are existing 1280-pixel uncropped Commons display variants; no local pixel edits were made. Source and license links accompany each. Stain and calibrated scale are not reported and are not inferred from colour. The photographs are separate specimens, not a time series. Full-size visual inspection found no visible patient names or record numbers. The display variants omit the original device identifiers. The cumulus variant retains exactly 56 EXIF bytes: a blank ImageDescription and YCbCrPositioning=1; the metadata policy permits only those exact bytes for that asset. Other metadata remains rejected.

Human developmental and adult spatial atlases are distinguished from mouse interventions. The PTEN, KIT-coupling and FOXL2 examples retain their stage, intervention and model limits. Human retinoid experiments distinguish transcript induction from completed meiosis. The 2024 atlas is reference-only: no noncommercial or BioRender-containing figure is reproduced. Conflicting numerical counts in source prose/captions are not imported. Restricted source artwork, research figures and videos are excluded.

Source and editorial review is AI-assisted; independent medical specialist review remains outstanding. Matched atresia/corpus-luteum photographs and comprehensive reproductive endocrinology/pathology remain incomplete. Build, interaction, accessibility, mobile, image failure, print, downloads, exact-preview and production verification are release gates. No patient diagnosis or automatic free-text grade is provided.
