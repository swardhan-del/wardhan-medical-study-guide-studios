# Histology Foundations guided depth — 7 November 2026

Students could complete the nine introductory questions without finding the deeper preparation, tissue-architecture, mechanism and developmental-evidence questions already available in other lessons. The new four-stage path references 24 existing questions in six lessons and adds return navigation. Question records, answer keys and learner-progress identifiers remain unchanged.

The accompanying three-prompt research exercise is an original, invented scenario about interpreting antibody staining. It separates a western-blot result from validation in fixed-tissue immunohistochemistry, distinguishes an omission control from evidence about target specificity, and limits the interpretation of independent-antibody and RNA comparisons. It is self-review with model explanations, not automatic grading or clinical validation. Existing lesson notes provide optional writing and browser-local persistence.

The case cites Edfors et al. (2018), [western-blot validation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175901/); Sivertsson et al. (2020), [tissue immunohistochemistry](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7723238/); and Uhlén et al. (2016), [a working-group proposal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10335836/). The proposal is labelled separately from primary experiments. Article metadata, abstracts and relevant indexed primary-text sections were checked. This is not a comprehensive methods or supplementary-data review, and independent specialist review remains outstanding.
