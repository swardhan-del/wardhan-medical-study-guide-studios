# Visual study studio: source, visual and interaction review

## Teaching and source boundaries

Three original learning activities reuse existing microscopy and lymphoid-organ concepts. They add an explicit visual first approach without duplicating lesson identities, question banks or saved-progress registries. All 263 lesson objects and the 148 previously released public assets remain unchanged.

Lymph-node claims were cross-checked against the Duke University lymphatic practical and Brelje/Sorenson's Histology Guide lymph-node page. The capsule–sinus relationship, follicular outer cortex, deeper paracortex, medullary cords/sinuses, afferent/efferent lymphatics and HEV blood entry are retained as distinct structures. A follicle is not an organ-specific diagnosis; an HEV is a blood vessel, and the paracortex is not a lymph sinus. The selected blood and lymph paths are conceptual examples, without transit times or measured geometry.

Spleen/thymus comparison uses the Duke practical and Histology Guide's neonatal/adult thymus descriptions. White pulp is arteriole-associated within red pulp; it is not a cortical band. Thymic septa incompletely partition the organ, medullary tissue connects, and Hassall corpuscles are epithelial structures rather than germinal centres. The drawings cannot classify an uploaded or cropped clinical specimen.

Paraffin preparation is checked against Southern Illinois University's tissue-preparation account and the retained source-reviewed microscopy lesson. Processing/support precede cutting; conventional aqueous H&E follows wax removal and rehydration. Finishing varies by method. The activity gives purposes, not reagent/time instructions; frozen and other methods differ. H&E colour alone does not identify a tissue.

Three retained course/guide PDFs were compared privately for scope and corresponding structural relationships. Exact source identities, hashes and selected page evidence are held outside the public code. No source passage, lecture image, atlas tile, measured research dataset, examination-bank item or personal/patient information is reproduced. Public references are linked adjacent to the learning activities.

## Authored assets and review findings

Nine original programmed schematic SVGs cover the three organ patterns and six preparation stages. A generated conceptual microscope illustration is decorative and explicitly distinguished from a specimen. Three original silent portrait MP4s animate the schematics with on-screen teaching, pause/predict prompts where appropriate, WebVTT captions and complete transcripts. The exact 19 new asset hashes are appended to the existing public-release allowlist; no previous asset record is replaced. The generator and scientific text are retained for reproducibility.

Visual review corrected an HEV that initially overlapped a cortical follicle: it now lies in the model's deeper cortex. The illustrated lymph path now enters the medullary sinus region before leaving at the hilum. Red-pulp pattern marks are drawn beneath white-pulp islands. On small phones, static numbered markers correspond to full-size labelled buttons rather than overlapping touch targets. There is no automatic animation or media playback.
