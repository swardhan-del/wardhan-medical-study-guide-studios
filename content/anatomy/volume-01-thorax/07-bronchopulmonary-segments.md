## Locate the structures

A bronchopulmonary segment is a subdivision supplied by a segmental bronchus and pulmonary arterial branch. Right-sided descriptions commonly list ten segments; left-sided descriptions may combine segments, so counts vary by convention. Segment borders are not always visible as fissures. Distinguish this internal bronchovascular organization from the larger lobar divisions.

## Connect structure and function

Pulmonary arteries carry right-ventricular blood toward gas-exchange capillaries and pulmonary veins return blood to the left atrium. Bronchial arteries provide systemic nutritive supply. Superficial subpleural and deep peribronchial lymphatic networks drain toward pulmonary and bronchopulmonary nodes, then tracheobronchial pathways. Cross-drainage can occur, so lymphatic territories should not be treated as completely isolated lobar compartments.

## Why are segmental pulmonary arteries and intersegmental veins arranged differently?

Bronchi and pulmonary arterial branches distribute together toward the gas-exchanging region within a segment. Venous channels collect from adjacent territories and often follow boundaries between segments. This difference supports the concept of a segment as a bronchovascular unit while explaining why venous drainage is not confined to a matching central bundle. On a schematic, draw two neighboring segments so that the shared boundary vein is visible; one isolated segment diagram can conceal that relationship.

## Apply the anatomy and resolve a common confusion

A segment has a segmental bronchus and accompanying pulmonary arterial branch, while pulmonary veins commonly follow intersegmental planes. This arrangement explains why a segment is a useful anatomical unit. Pulmonary circulation carries blood for gas exchange; bronchial circulation supplies conducting tissues through systemic branches. Some bronchial venous blood returns through pulmonary veins, so drainage is not wholly separated into two simple circuits. Lobar number alone does not tell you the number of segments.
