## Locate the structures

The SA node lies near the SVC-right atrial junction. The AV node is located in the inferior right atrial septal region near landmarks of the triangle of Koch. The AV bundle passes into the interventricular septal region and divides into right and left branches before distributing through the ventricular conduction network.

## Connect structure and function

Sympathetic cardiac input generally increases rate, conduction and contractile force; vagal input slows nodal activity. Visceral pain afferents often accompany sympathetic pathways to upper thoracic spinal levels, contributing to referred pain patterns. These afferents do not become sympathetic motor fibers merely by sharing a route. Electrical excitation spreads through specialized cardiac cells rather than conventional somatic motor nerves.

## Why can autonomic input alter rate without acting like a skeletal motor nerve?

Cardiac pacemaker and conduction cells have intrinsic electrical properties that generate and distribute impulses. Sympathetic and parasympathetic inputs modify those properties, changing rate and conduction rather than triggering each contraction through a somatic neuromuscular junction. The myocardium then contracts through excitation-contraction coupling. This explains why the anatomical conduction system remains central even when autonomic influence changes. Distinguish pacemaker initiation, conduction timing and contractile response rather than treating them as one electrical event.

## Apply the anatomy and resolve a common confusion

The sinoatrial node initiates the usual rhythm, atrial conduction reaches the AV node, and the AV bundle continues into bundle branches and Purkinje fibers. The AV nodal delay helps coordinate ventricular filling before ventricular activation. Autonomic input changes rate and conduction but does not normally supply a new motor impulse for each heartbeat. The right bundle has fibers associated with the moderator band. Electrical pathway anatomy should be distinguished from coronary arterial supply even though ischemia can affect conduction.
